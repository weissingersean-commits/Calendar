# Homelight Setup Guide

**Homelight** is a free family calendar that runs right in your web browser — no account needed, no app store, no subscription.

---

## Step 1 — Open the App

On your phone or tablet, open **Chrome** and go to:

**https://weissingersean-commits.github.io/Calendar/**

---

## Step 2 — Install It (Recommended)

Installing makes it feel like a real app with no browser bar.

1. In Chrome, tap the **three dots menu (⋮)** in the top right
2. Tap **Add to Home Screen**
3. Tap **Add**

It will now appear on your home screen like any other app.

---

## Step 3 — Connect Your Google Calendar

This syncs your existing Google Calendar events into Homelight automatically.

**You'll need to do this on a computer (not your phone):**

1. Open **Google Calendar** at calendar.google.com
2. Click the ⚙ **gear icon** in the top right → click **Settings**
3. On the left side, click your calendar name (under "Settings for my calendars")
4. Scroll down until you see **"Integrate calendar"**
5. Find **"Secret address in iCal format"** and click **Copy**

**Now back in Homelight (phone or tablet):**

6. Tap the **📅 calendar icon** in the top bar
7. Tap **+ Add Calendar Feed**
8. Give it a name (e.g. "My Google Calendar")
9. Paste the link you copied
10. Tap **Add**

Your events will appear within a minute. It refreshes automatically every 15 minutes.

> **Note:** Events from Google Calendar are view-only in Homelight. To add or change events, do it in Google Calendar — it will update in Homelight on the next sync.

---

## Step 4 — Connect Your Apple Calendar (iCloud)

**On your iPhone or Mac:**

1. Open the **Calendar** app
2. Tap the calendar you want to share (tap the ⓘ info button next to it)
3. Turn on **Public Calendar**
4. Tap **Share Link** and copy it

**In Homelight:**

5. Tap **📅** → **+ Add Calendar Feed**
6. Give it a name and paste the link
7. Tap **Add**

---

## Adding Events Manually

- Tap **+ Event** in the top right corner
- Or tap any time slot in the **Day** or **Week** view to add an event at that time
- Fill in the title, date, time, and how long it lasts
- Tap **Add Event**

---

## Getting Around

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| Switch views | Tap **Day / Week / Month** at the top |
| Go forward or back | **Swipe left or right** on the calendar |
| Jump to a specific day | Tap any day in Month view |
| See today | Tap the **Today** button |
| Search for an event | Tap **🔍 Search** in the top bar |

---

## Other Features Worth Knowing

- **Tasks & Grocery List** — in the right sidebar, switch between Tasks, Grocery, and Meals tabs
- **Screensaver** — tap **🖼** in the top bar to test it; set a timer in Settings to have it turn on automatically
- **Weather** — shows automatically based on your location
- **Hide the sidebar** — tap the **▶ button** in the top right corner to give the calendar more space

---

## Settings

Tap the **⚙ gear icon** in the top bar to open Settings.

From there you can change the theme (dark or light), set which day the week starts on, add screensaver photos, and turn on event reminders.
